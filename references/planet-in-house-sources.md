# Planet-in-house: pages actually opened

Fetched 2026-11-16. Keys match `planet-in-house.json`.

## Classical texts (primary)

- **PD8** https://www.wisdomlib.org/hinduism/book/phaladeepika-by-mantreswara-text-and-translation/d/doc1621580.html — Phaladeepika ch. 8 (V. Subrahmanya Sastri tr.). Moon through Ketu in all 12 houses. This page leaves out Sun in houses 1-7, and its OCR has not been proofread.
- **PD8s** https://www.siva.sh/phaladeepika/8 — Phaladeepika ch. 8, verses 1-5. Supplies Sun in houses 1-12, which the wisdomlib page lacks, and agrees with it for Moon in houses 1-3.
- **SAR** https://lakshminarayanlenasia.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/10/docs2/LakshmiNarayanLenasia.com/Astrology (Jyotish) collection/Saravali (Kalyana Varma).pdf — Saravali ch. 30, "Effects of Planets in Bhavas". Full English text for Sun to Saturn in houses 1-12. Saravali does not cover Rahu or Ketu.
- **BS** https://lakshminarayanlenasia.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/10/docs2/LakshmiNarayanLenasia.com/Astrology (Jyotish) collection/Bhrigu Sutras (partial significance of the Planets and Houses).pdf — Bhrigu Sutras with a modern commentary. Covers all 9 planets in the 12 houses, and gives Rahu and Ketu the same effects in each house.
- **CC** (same PDF as BS) — Chamatkar Chintamani's Rahu and Ketu readings for each house. These are only as quoted in the BS commentary, so they are secondhand.
- https://wisdomlib.org/hinduism/book/phaladeepika-by-mantreswara-text-and-translation — the Phaladeepika table of contents. Used only to find the chapter 8 page.
- https://archive.org/stream/ParasharaHoraSastra/BrihatParasharaHoraSastraVedicAstrologyEbook_djvu.txt — full text of BPHS (Brihat Parashara Hora Shastra). Chapters 12-23 judge houses through house lords and combinations, not through planet-by-house lists. Not cited for any entry.

## Modern / South Indian cross-checks

- **AV** https://www.astroved.com/blogs/effects-of-rahu-in-12-houses — AstroVed (Tamil-tradition company): Rahu's good and bad effects in each house.
- **AVk** https://www.astroved.com/blogs/ketu-in-12-houses-of-kundali — AstroVed: Ketu's good and bad effects in each house.
- **VF-Ma** https://vedicfeed.com/effects-of-mars-in-12-houses/ — Mars in each house (modern and psychological in tone).
- **VF-Sa** https://vedicfeed.com/saturn-effects-in-12-houses/ — Saturn in each house.
- **VF-Ve** https://vedicfeed.com/venus-effects-in-12-houses/ — Venus in each house. Loosely edited.
- **VF-Ra** https://vedicfeed.com/effects-of-rahu-in-12-houses/ — Rahu in each house.
- **VF-Ke** https://vedicfeed.com/ketu-in-12-houses-of-kundali/ — Ketu in each house (Lal Kitab-leaning).
- **EV** https://theeyeofvedas.blogspot.com/2025/11/effects-of-rahu-kethu-in-different-bhavas.html — Rahu and Ketu read as an axis across the houses. Cites no source.
- **VAW** https://vedic-astrology-wiki.blogspot.com/2025/08/planets-in-different-houses.html — a one-line table for all 9 planets in all 12 houses, drawn from "traditional texts" it does not name. This is the only page besides BS with one-line readings for every planet; PD8 also covers all 9 but in prose.
- **PB** https://panchangbodh.com/sun-in-houses — Sun in each house, with notes on what BPHS assigns the Sun.
- **DM** https://dailymirror.lk/jyotisha-news/Effects-Venus-produces-in-the-Bhavas/312-151033 — Daily Mirror (Sri Lanka) jyotisha column: Venus in each house.
