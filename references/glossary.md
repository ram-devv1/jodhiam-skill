The 12 signs from Mesham are: Mesham, Rishabam, Mithunam, Kadagam, Simmam, Kanni, Thulam,
Viruchigam, Dhanusu, Magaram, Kumbam, Meenam. In the South Indian box, Meenam is the top-left
cell and the signs run clockwise.
