## 1. The method: how Tamil jothidars get from chart to குணம்

### 1.1 What each teacher actually does

**Eshwar Gunasekaran, lagna videos (aFlH, h5Gw, ZxqG, WwYn, M0SQ)**

Lagna sign first, then the lagna lord's nature, then house rulerships, then planets in or aspecting the lagna. No navamsa, no lagna nakshatra.

- **A. Sign nature, the biggest weight.** Each label of the sign maps to a trait:
  - Element:
    - Fire: "ஏதாச்சு ஒரு வேலையை செய்து கொண்டே இருக்க வேண்டும்" (must always be doing some work) (aFlH).
    - Water: travel and changes of place (WwYn).
    - Air: likes change (ZxqG).
  - Movable / fixed / dual:
    - Fixed: "ஸ்திரமான புத்தி" (a steady mind) (h5Gw).
    - Dual: two minds, two plans, often two marriages (ZxqG).
  - Male or female sign: a female sign gives softness, even in men (h5Gw).
  - Symbol: the lion is solitary, so Simmam likes being alone (M0SQ).
  - Kalapurusha body part and house number:
    - Rishabam = 2nd house = face, food, speech, so a lovely face and a sweet tooth (h5Gw).
    - Simmam = 5th = stomach and intellect (M0SQ).
  - Terrain: Simmam = hills, so the native loves hill trips (M0SQ).
  - Which planets are exalted or debilitated in the sign:
    - Sun exalted in Mesham gives leadership (aFlH).
    - No planet exalted or debilitated there gives "ஒரே சீரான பலன்" (steady, even results) (ZxqG, M0SQ).
- **B. The lagna lord's own nature colours everything.**
  - Venus: beauty, sweets, clothes, small pleasures (சிற்றின்பம்), garden, pets, wanting to be unique, mild laziness (h5Gw).
  - Mars: hurry, temper, fearlessness, uniformed service (aFlH).
  - Mercury: cleverness, overthinking (ZxqG).
  - Moon: caring, imaginative (WwYn).
  - Sun: pride, perfectionism, independence (M0SQ).
- **C. House rulerships read as personality.**
  - Mesham's lagna lord is also the 8th lord: "succeeds on the second or later attempt" (aFlH).
  - A strong 2nd lord means an authoritative speaker (WwYn, M0SQ).
- **D. Functional rules, used mostly for ranking dasas.**
  - Badhaka house:
    - Movable lagna: 11th (aFlH, WwYn).
    - Fixed lagna: 9th (h5Gw, M0SQ).
    - Dual lagna: 7th (ZxqG).
  - **Hiding rule:** a planet sitting 6, 8 or 12 from a bad house it owns doesn't give that house's harm. "எட்டுக்கு எட்டாக மறையிறாரு… கெடுதலை செய்ய மாட்டார்" (if it hides 8th from the 8th, it won't harm) (aFlH).
  - **Split-dasa rule:** a planet owning two houses gives the first half of its dasa for one house and the second half for the other (ZxqG, WwYn, M0SQ).
  - Digbala (directional strength) rescues a weak dasa lord (h5Gw, ZxqG, WwYn).
  - Rahu and Ketu act like the lord of their sign, their star lord, and whatever joins or aspects them. "ராகு கொடுத்து கெடுப்பாரு, கேது ஆரம்பத்தில இருந்தே கெடுப்பாரு" (Rahu gives, then ruins; Ketu ruins from the start) (h5Gw, WwYn).
- **E. Planets in the lagna modify the base nature** (M0SQ):
  - Mars: violent anger.
  - Saturn: slow, a "slow pick-up".
  - Rahu: grandiose desire that ruins ("ஆசையே அழிவு", desire is their ruin).
  - Ketu: detachment and boredom.
  - A benefic aspect hides the anger but doesn't remove it: "உள்ளுக்குள்ள நெருப்பு" (fire inside) (aFlH).
- **F. Conjunctions with the lagna lord:** e.g. Mars+Saturn or Mars+Venus give character and marriage results (aFlH).
- **Weighting, as observed:** about 70% sign nature plus lagna lord nature in the character sections. The dasa sections run almost entirely on rulership.
- He uses "default" percentages freely: 70% of Rishabam natives are fair, 70% of Mithunam natives marry twice. Every video ends with "ஒரு ஜெனரல் காம்பினேஷன்… மாறுபடும்" (this is a general combination; it varies) (ZxqG).

**Eshwar Gunasekaran, nakshatra videos (nOQi, 4Wic, O8lC)**

Star lord, then rasi lord, then pada (navamsa) lord, read as one blend. The pada lord sets the tone within a pada.

1. **Star lord first, the base.** "சூரியனுடைய ஆதிக்கம் உங்களுக்கு முழுமையா இருக்கும் ... இது ஒரு பேஸ்" (the Sun's dominance is total; this is the base) (O8lC). Karthigai spans two rasis but gets the same star results, so the star lord outranks the rasi lord.
2. **Rasi lord second.** Blended with the star lord: "செவ்வாய் மற்றும் கேது இந்த இரண்டு கிரகத்துடைய ஆதிக்கத்துக்குள்ள" (under the sway of these two planets, Mars and Ketu) (nOQi). Each keyword gets a plus and a minus: Mars speed means quick decisions, but also "யோசிக்காம அவசரப்பட்ட முடிவுகள்" (rash decisions made without thinking).
3. **Pada = navamsa sign and its lord.** "ராசிக்கு அதிபதி ... நட்சத்திர அதிபதி ... நட்சத்திர பாத அதிபதி ... இந்த மூன்று கிரகத்துடைய ஆதிக்கம்" (the influence of these three planets: rasi lord, star lord, pada lord) (4Wic, O8lC).
   - The pada lord dominates pada-level traits: "மேஜரா பார்க்கும்போது குருபகவானுடைய அம்சம்" (taken as a whole, it is Jupiter's character) (O8lC).
   - A pada can override the star's general result: Ashwini overall has few friends, but pada 2 has many (nOQi).
   - A repeated lord intensifies: Bharani pada 4 = Mars + Venus + Mars, so the Mars traits are strong (4Wic).
4. **The navamsa sign's gender changes how the same lord behaves** (O8lC):
   - Kumbam (male) Saturn: greed, money-hunger, delayed revenge.
   - Makaram (female) Saturn: melts for affection, gets betrayed.
   - Dhanus (male) Jupiter: strategic, loves praise.
   - Meenam (female) Jupiter: detached, devout, compassionate.
5. **Deity and symbol, sometimes** (Karthigai only): Agni gives intolerance of injustice; the knife gives "வெட்டு ஒன்னு துண்டு ரெண்டு" (blunt, cut-and-dried speech).
6. **Moon check, the only chart-level gate.**
   - "சுய ஜாதகத்தில சந்திர பகவான் பாதிக்கப்படாம இருக்கணும்" (in your own chart, the Moon must be unafflicted). Then the reading fits 70–80%. If the Moon is afflicted, 50–60% is already a lot (4Wic).
   - Saturn or Rahu touching the Moon spoils the reading. Ketu with the Moon is fine for a Ketu star (nOQi).
7. **Not used:** lagna, dasa, gana, yoni, animal.
8. **Venus decides love marriage:** "சுக்கிரன் நல்ல நிலையில் இருந்தால் மட்டும்தான்" (only if Venus is well placed) (nOQi).

**Gokulakannan (ELAI, 31Nd, XfPK), Pulippani siddhar line, quotes ஜாதக அலங்காரம் (Jataka Alankaram)**
- Lagna is central. His order: lagna, then the lagna lord's state, then planets aspecting the lagna, then planets joined with the lagna lord. "லக்னத்தை மையமாக வைத்து… லக்னாதிபதி இருக்கும் நிலை, லக்னத்தை பார்த்த கிரகங்கள், லக்னாதிபதியோடு சேர்ந்த கிரகங்கள்" (centred on the lagna: the lagna lord's state, planets aspecting the lagna, planets joined with the lagna lord) (ELAI).
- Transits are secondary.
- A weak lagna lord ruins every other yoga. Weak means "6/8/12ல மறைஞ்சிட்டார், பாவ கிரகங்களோட சேர்ந்துட்டார், நீச்சம், அஸ்தங்கம், தசாபலன் கை கொடுக்கல" (hidden in 6/8/12, joined with malefics, debilitated, combust, or its dasa not supporting) (ELAI).
- He uses sign traits to settle lagna-sandhi doubts: whoever behaves like Thulam is Thulam (ELAI).
- Sathayam: Kumbam as the 11th sign (fixed, a gains sign), then star lord Rahu as the main driver, then Saturn as rasi lord and Rahu's friend ("டபுள் பெனிபிட்", a double benefit), then the classical verses (31Nd).
- **Rahu's house decides how the character shows:** "அவர் இருக்கிற வீட்டுக்கு தகுந்த மாதிரிதான் குணமே முதல் வெளிப்படும்" (the character shows first according to the house he sits in) (XfPK).
- Then the dasa sequence, with tara (taraabalam) for each dasa lord counted from the pada (XfPK).
- Quoting Pulippani: "சும்மா ஒரு கிரகத்தை வச்சு முடிவு பண்ணாதயா லக்னாதிபதி எப்படி இருக்கிறார்" (don't decide from one planet; see how the lagna lord stands) (XfPK).

**Yogi Jayaprakash (UIE5)**
- The sign explains character, and the lagna counts for about 40%: "இது 40 சதமானம்… வேற கிரகங்க சம்பந்தப்பட்ட மாறவும் செய்யும்" (this is 40%; it changes with other planets).
- Dasa results come from functional benefic and malefic status.
- The janma rasi decides when the good dasas arrive in life.

**Sriramji (sgsl)**
- Thulam is split into its 9 navamsa padas. Each pada is read by which house the navamsa sign counts to from the lagna, plus its sign lord and star lord.

**Rajeshwari Chellaiah (sz3w, zfAv)**
- Works almost entirely from the sign and Saturn's rulership, by contrasting Kumbam with Makaram.
- Same lord, different result: in Makaram, Mars is exalted, so the native's labour pays them back; in Kumbam it goes to others.
- Uses the sign's image: "குடத்தில் விட்ட விளக்கு" (a lamp inside a pot, i.e. hidden, backstage).
- Karma reading: possibly the last birth.

**Mahesh Iyer (4XI3)**
- Keeps rasi and lagna separate and gives two trait blocks.
- Starts from the pot image (it attracts, so magnetism), then reads the three stars.
- When the sign's promise fails (e.g. money), check the 2nd house of the birth chart.

**Combination teachers**
- One2one, Rajendran (NY3):
  - Starts from each planet's natural meaning.
  - Says friendship or enmity between the two barely matters.
  - Any link between them counts: conjunction, aspect, or one in the other's sign.
  - Needs both planets strong and not in the 6, 8 or 12.
- Avinashi Jothilingam (ZApm):
  - Starts from natural meaning: Moon = mind (மனசு), Mercury = intellect (புத்தி).
  - An actual conjunction gives 100% of the effect. The Moon in a Mercury star (Ayilyam, Kettai, Revathi) gives about 50%.
- Parthipan (tocu), nadi style:
  - Jupiter = the native, Saturn = his karma.
  - Saturn in the 1st, 5th, 7th or 9th from Jupiter gives contentment.
  - Rejects the "Guru Brahmahathi dosham" label.
- Aditya Guruji (ACVH):
  - Benefic vs malefic influence on a house (சுபத்துவம் / பாபத்துவம்), plus subtle strength for planets (சூட்சும வலு).
  - Every house is mixed, roughly 60/40.
  - A planet in the 8th aspects the 2nd, which gives the speech and family results.
- Dhanasekar (mw-A):
  - Reads a planet in the 8th through its star lord. It does well in the star of the 2nd, 5th, 9th, 10th or 11th lord.
  - Debility needs cancellation (நீசபங்கம்).
  - Jupiter may be hidden from the lagna, but not from the Moon.
- Bakthi Infinity, Rahu in the 9th (CkJU):
  - House meaning plus Rahu's aspects (3rd, 7th, 11th), modified by planets joining or aspecting it.
  - "பாவிகள் எவ்வளவு தோஷம் கொடுக்கறாங்களோ அதுக்கு ட்ரிபிள் மடங்கு யோகம்" (malefics give triple the yoga for the dosha they give).

### 1.2 Common method, in order of weight

Combined from the teachers above, for writing a behaviours section:

1. **Lagna sign nature.** Element, movable/fixed/dual, gender, symbol, Kalapurusha house and body part, exalted and debilitated planets there. Used by Eshwar, Gokulakannan, Jayaprakash, Mahesh Iyer. This gives the outer behaviour and lifestyle defaults.
2. **Lagna lord.** Its own nature first. Then its strength and placement: kendra/kona good; 6, 8, 12 hidden; dignity; combust; malefic company. Gokulakannan treats a weak lagna lord as overriding everything. Eshwar treats its nature as the colour of the whole personality.
3. **Planets in or aspecting the lagna, and planets joined with the lagna lord.** Malefics in the lagna sharpen or spoil the temperament. A benefic aspect softens it on the surface (aFlH, M0SQ, ELAI).
4. **Moon: rasi plus nakshatra plus pada.** This is the inner mind. Star lord, then rasi lord, then pada (navamsa) lord, with the pada lord leading at pada level. Valid only to the extent the Moon is unafflicted (Eshwar). Rajeshwari, Mahesh Iyer and Gokulakannan give rasi and star portraits independent of the lagna. Mahesh Iyer explicitly keeps rasi and lagna separate.
5. **Specific combinations and placements:** conjunctions, planets in houses, Rahu's sign. Read by natural meaning, then house meaning, then rulership, then dignity, star lord and aspects. These are add-ons to the base portrait.
6. **Dasa sequence:** how the traits unfold over life, e.g. the Sathayam Rahu → Guru → Sani → Budhan arc (XfPK, Hnut). The character is the same, but different facets dominate in each period.
7. **Hedge.** Every teacher frames this as a majority tendency and asks viewers to compare it with their own life.

Points of disagreement on method:
- **Lagna vs nakshatra weight.** Gokulakannan and Jayaprakash are lagna-first. Eshwar's star videos ignore the lagna entirely. Mahesh Iyer gives both, separately. **Practical rule:** describe outward behaviour and lifestyle from the lagna, and inner temperament and emotional style from the Moon rasi and star. Say so in the output.
- **Navamsa.** Eshwar's lagna videos never use it. His star videos use the navamsa only as the "pada lord". Sriramji uses the lagna's navamsa pada heavily (sgsl). None of the Sathayam teachers name the navamsa for any pada.
- **Enmity in conjunctions.** NY3 says it barely matters. Eshwar notes Mercury treats the Moon as an enemy while the Moon treats Mercury as a friend (ZxqG).

## 2. Thulam (Libra) lagnam

Sources: ELAI (Gokulakannan), UIE5 (Jayaprakash), sgsl (Sriramji). Eshwar has no Thulam video. Carry-overs from his Venus-ruled Rishabam video (h5Gw) are marked.

### 2.1 Sign facts the teachers use
- Venus's moolatrikona sign. 7th house of the Kalapurusha. Saturn exalted, Sun debilitated: "அதிகாரம் எல்லாம் இங்க செல்லாது" (authority doesn't work here) (sgsl).
- "ஜடலக்னம்": an inanimate lagna, because the symbol (the scales) is an object (sgsl).
- "தொழில் அதிபர்களை உருவாக்கக்கூடிய… வீடு" (a house that makes industrialists) (sgsl).
- Two opposite natures inside one person, Saturn and Venus: "இந்த வேலைய முடிச்சிட்டு வந்துறேன் அப்படின்னா அது சனி… நான் பீச்சுக்கு போயிட்டு வரேன்னா அது சுக்கிரன்" ("I'll finish this work first" is Saturn; "I'm off to the beach" is Venus) (UIE5).
- Holds a full Rahu star (Swathi), like Mithunam and Kumbam. Rahu "provokes and scatters" (sgsl).
- Movable sign. [extrapolation from Eshwar's movable-sign rule, aFlH/WwYn] The badhaka lord is the 11th lord, the Sun; Jayaprakash independently names the Sun as badhakadhipathi (UIE5). The maraka is Mars, lord of the 2nd and 7th.

## 3. Sathayam (Shatabhisha), including pada 4

Sources: 31Nd and XfPK (Gokulakannan), eZcH (Life Horoscope), Hnut (Rajayogam), 4XI3 (Mahesh Iyer, star within Kumbam). Eshwar's method from 1.1 is used for the pada-4 extrapolation.

### 3.4 Pada 4 (Meenam navamsa, Jupiter)

What the teachers actually say:
- **eZcH (Life Horoscope), the only explicit pada-4 trait:** "எந்த சூழ்நிலையும் எந்த இடத்திலும்… நான் கீழ இருந்து மேல வருவேன் அப்படின்னு ப்ரூவ் பண்றதுதான் சதயம் பாதம் நான்கு. ஆரம்பத்துல ரொம்ப சாதாரணம் ரொம்ப லோ லெவல் அப்படியே மேல கொண்டு வந்துரும்" (whatever the situation or place, Sathayam pada 4 proves "I will rise from the bottom". It starts very ordinary, very low, and lifts them up). He gives no logic, no navamsa reasoning.
  - His other padas, for contrast: pada 1 makes a name, "writes it on a board"; pada 2 brings cash flow, commanding speech, business; pada 3 wants to experience and enjoy everything.
- **None of the four Sathayam teachers names the navamsa sign or lord for any pada.**

Built with Eshwar's method [extrapolation]: rasi lord Saturn + star lord Rahu + pada lord Jupiter in a female navamsa sign (Meenam). The pada lord leads at pada level (O8lC).
- His only Meenam-navamsa model is **Karthigai pada 4** (O8lC). Female-sign Jupiter is "ரொம்ப அலாதியான பர்சன்" (a very unusual person) and "குருவுடைய முழுமையான தன்மையும் இந்த பாதத்தில் உள்ளவர்கள்ட்ட தான் வெளிப்படும்" (Jupiter's full nature shows only in this pada). That pada is:
  - Detached: "நீ நல்லது செஞ்சாலும் ... கெட்டது செஞ்சாலும் பிரச்சனை இல்லை ... எனக்கு தேவை நிம்மதி" (whether you do me good or harm, I don't mind; what I need is peace).
  - Charitable, a good adviser, teaching or guiding work.
  - Compassion that others exploit, which they shrug off.
  - Disciplined, starts the day with prayer.
  - Melts for affection and gets cheated, but gives pure love.
  - Picks good friends, harbours no ill-will.
- Applying that layer to Sathayam: the Rahu/Saturn base (intuition, secrecy, stubbornness, temper, foreign links, rising from the bottom) is softened toward the Jupiter pole: dharma, advising, devotion, detachment, generosity that gets exploited.
- This fits two direct Sathayam sources: generosity to the point of loss (31Nd, Hnut) and the moksha/mukti theme (31Nd, XfPK).
- It also fits eZcH's "rise from the bottom". Pada 4 gets Guru dasa in childhood (3.3), so discipline and wisdom come early.
- Eshwar's Moon rule applies: the portrait holds better if Saturn and Rahu don't aspect or join the Kumbam Moon (nOQi, 4Wic).

  - "போட்டி இருக்கும் வெற்றி இருக்கும்" (there will be competition, and there will be victory).
  - Success after hard struggle, "கடுமையான போட்டிகள் வம்பு வழக்குகள் பொறாமைகள்" (fierce competition, quarrels, lawsuits, jealousy).
  - Foreign life brings growth, but "விரயத்தை கட்டுப்படுத்த முடியாது" (expenses can't be controlled).
  - "உடல் ஆரோக்கியம் எதிரிகள் கடன் விஷயத்தில் கவனமா" (careful about health, enemies, debts).
  - This is navamsa, not rasi. Use by analogy only.
- -aX, Venus in the 6th in general: "மனைவி வேலை செஞ்சுட்டு இருப்பாங்க" (the wife will be working) and "பெண்ணுதான் எதிரி" (a woman is the enemy).
- ACVH: Venus is not "hidden" in the 6th. It aspects the 12th (the house of pleasures) and raises virility; the native is liked by women. Exaltation is not discussed.
- Rule conflict:
  - ELAI: a lagna lord in the 6, 8 or 12 is weak. He doesn't say whether exaltation cancels it.
  - Eshwar's hiding rule (h5Gw): a lagna lord that also owns the 6th gives yoga when it sits 6, 8 or 12 from the 6th.
  - [extrapolation] For Thulam, Venus is the 1st and 8th lord, so the relevant test is the 8th. The 6th from the lagna is the 11th from the 8th, so the hiding rule does not clear the 8th-lord role.
  - **Synthesis to write:** comforts and work through service, competition and effort. Wins over rivals. Spends freely. Watch health, debts and quarrels. Possibly a working spouse.

### 5.4 Jupiter + Saturn together, and in the 8th
- Together (tocu):
  - நிறைவு (contentment): "இருக்கறத வச்சிட்டு சந்தோஷப்படுறாங்க" (they are glad with what they have). Buoyant through losses, lives in the present, carefree.
  - Detached from relatives, but has a big circle of genuine friends. Helps others, even when asked rudely.
  - "Face value": strangers think they know them; respected beyond their qualifications.
  - Takes responsibility and starts earning early (leaves home at 17), which he calls the only drawback.
  - Body: leg itching and leg injuries.
  - Saturn in the 1st, 5th, 7th or 9th from Jupiter gives a contented life. "Guru Brahmahathi dosham" is "ஒன்னும் கிடையாது" (nothing at all). Also called Dharma-Karmadhipati yoga.
- **In the 8th, together:** not discussed by any source.
- Jupiter in the 8th:
  - ACVH: "குரு எட்டில் மறையவே கூடாது" (Jupiter must never hide in the 8th); it hurts children and male heirs, but gives foreign residence and distant travel.
  - mw-A: "தாராளமா இருக்கலாம்" (perfectly fine). Good education, unusual thinking, deep analysis, as long as it isn't debilitated and isn't in the 6, 8 or 12 from the Moon.
  - **They disagree.**
- Saturn in the 8th:
  - Long life, "தீர்க்க ஆயுள்" (ACVH, mw-A agree).
  - ACVH: if malefic, "ஆயுளை மட்டும் அதிகப்படுத்தி இன்னும் பிற அத்தனை விஷயங்களையும் கெடுப்பார்" (lengthens only life and spoils everything else). Aspecting the 2nd and 10th gives lawyer or clever-speech careers.
  - mw-A: "overall average". Results by lagna and star lord.
- General 8th-house character:
  - Longevity, secrecy, occult and hidden research, Vedanta (ACVH).
  - Sudden gains and losses, other people's money, foreign residence (ACVH, mw-A).
  - Inheritance trouble (mw-A, for Mars).
- [extrapolation] **For Thulam lagna**, the 8th is Rishabam (Venus). Jupiter is the 3rd and 6th lord, the functional malefic (UIE5). Saturn is the yogakaraka (ELAI, UIE5). Together in the 8th, the combination blends:
  - Contentment and early responsibility (tocu).
  - Long life (Saturn in the 8th).
  - Research and deep thinking (mw-A on Jupiter).
  - Some sudden ups and downs and hidden matters (8th-house nature).
  - Present it as tendencies, not certainties.

### 5.5 Rahu in the 9th (CkJU; aspects counted as 3rd, 7th and 11th)
- Overall: "அள்ளி அள்ளி கொடுப்பான்" (gives by the handful): fame and status after early hardship. Don't fear it.
- Beliefs: strong devotion. Female deities (Varahi), Bhairavar, Siddhars. Visits oracles, swamis and astrologers. Ignores Rahu kalam. The family often has a priest or astrologer, or a government link.
- Father: health scare or surgery, has been cheated, or has bad habits. Father's sister "not good".
- Education: late study gives yoga (law, medicine, astrology, an MBA after working).
- Foreign: gains through other places, states and languages. Study away from home recommended.
- Behaviour:
  - Honest and principled "to some extent". Early habits change after a lesson.
  - Fast walker, a healing or medical bent, drawn to government and politics.
  - Warnings: greed, gambling, shortcuts, fake documents. "இங்க தப்பு பண்ணா உள்ள போயருவேன்" (a mistake here lands you in jail).
- Rise from the day they become a parent, or when the child turns 9 or 11.
- Modifiers:
  - Saturn or Mars with Rahu: hard on the father, but "யோகத்தை குறைக்காது" (doesn't reduce the yoga).
  - Jupiter linked: "நினைச்சு பார்க்க முடியாத உச்சத்துல" (an unimaginable peak).
  - Venus or Moon strong: media.
  - Mercury strong: commission, writing.
  - Saturn strong: runs several businesses.
- Eshwar, general Rahu rule: acts like its sign lord, star lord and associates (WwYn). Rahu in the 9th or 12th gives earning far away (h5Gw, Rishabam).
- [extrapolation] For Thulam lagna, the 9th is Mithunam (Mercury, the 9th and 12th lord). By Eshwar's rule, Rahu there acts like Mercury: reasoning, trade, communication. This agrees with CkJU's "Mercury strong: commission, writing".

Common patterns:

- Each video ends with remedies (temple, weekday, deity). Optional for a behaviours section.

## 7. Applying it to the requested profile

Profile: Thulam lagnam, Sathayam pada 4 Moon in Kumbam, Moon+Mercury, Mars in the lagna, exalted Venus in the 6th, Jupiter+Saturn in the 8th, Rahu in the 9th. Every point is an extrapolation built from the cited items above. Write it hedged.

- **Outward manner (lagna):**
  - Fair-minded, balanced, aesthetic: clothes, perfume, a tidy home.
  - Prefers to run their own show, with delay before action (2.2).
  - Mars in the lagna adds speed, courage, sibling-protectiveness, a quick temper (5.2).
  - The ELAI/UIE5 split on bluntness tilts toward UIE5's "says it to your face" when Mars is in the lagna.
- **Inner temperament (Moon):**
  - Kumbam: works for others, backstage, family sacrifice, some fear and pessimism (4.1).
  - Sathayam: intuition, secrecy, stubborn decisions, resilience, "rise from the bottom" (3.2, 3.4).
  - Pada 4, Jupiter layer: detachment, devotion, advising, generosity that gets exploited (3.4).
  - Moon+Mercury: versatile, studious, wordsmith, anticipatory worry (5.1).
- **Money:** spends on comforts and others, and finds expenses hard to control (Venus in the 6th, Kumbam giver). The 4XI3 "frugal saver" view disagrees, so present both as a tension.
- **Work:** service and competition fields where they win by effort (Venus in the 6th). Execution and coordination (Kumbam). Thinking, research and intuition (Sathayam, 8th-house Jupiter). Communication and commission (Rahu in the 9th, Mercury's sign).
- **Relationships:**
  - Marriage is generally described as good for Kumbam (4.1).
  - Venus in the 6th: a working spouse (-aX).
  - Sathayam: risk of a self-chosen match or hidden faults if the Rahu bhukti in Guru dasa coincides (XfPK). For pada 4 that period falls in the 20s [extrapolation from 3.3].
- **Life arc:** Guru dasa comes early (pada 4). Saturn dasa is yogakaraka for Thulam, with "unschooled genius" potential (XfPK) and Saturn and Mercury dasas in the prime years (UIE5). Rahu in the 9th: rise after early hardship, devotion, foreign gains (5.5).
- **Health tendencies to mention lightly:**
  - Gastric, nerves, skin (Moon+Mercury, ZApm).
  - Blood pressure from temper (Sathayam, Hnut).
  - Health and debts (Venus in the 6th, sgsl).
  - Legs (Jupiter+Saturn, tocu).
- **Sleep and food:** thin evidence.
  - "Can't bear hunger" (31Nd, Sathayam).
  - Sleepless before anticipated events (ZApm).
  - Strong Saturn means quick sleep (ELAI).
  - Say no more than this.
