# Part A — Sri Mahalakshmi Jothidam (Sriramji), 6 videos

All six are by the same speaker (ஸ்ரீராம்ஜி, "Kalachakram" series), short (6–10 min) single-topic talks. ASR is clean enough; Tamil quotes copied as-is.
No classical text is quoted by name in any of the six; once he cites "மூல நூல்கள்" (root texts) generically (XAU7TjRG8Gk, Ketu = Mars rule).

Consistent house philosophy across videos (his weighting):
- A planet gives results of **the house it LORDS (ஆதிபத்தியம்) most**, then **the house it sits in (நின்ற வீடு)**, mixed with its **karakatvam**; that is ~90–95%. Nakshatra lord (சாரநாதன்) = ~5%.
- Whether lordship results come out good or bad is decided by **benefic contact (சுபக்கோள் தொடர்பு), contact with lagna-benefics, and lagna-lord strength**.

## 2. XAU7TjRG8Gk — "Is own star (சுயசாரம்) good?"

Gist: only the Moon really transmits nakshatra effects; for others own-star is judged by the *sign* the own-star falls in. Gives a % goodness per planet.

Principle (early): planets act on 3 levels: own nature, strength from the occupied sign, nakshatra; nakshatra least. Moon is the exception (mirror, nearest; janma nakshatra comes from it). Late: other planets act 95% by own nature + sign strength.
> "சந்திரன் மட்டுமே நட்சத்திர ஒளியின் தன்மையை மிக அதிகமாக பிரதிபலிக்கும்" / "95% செயல்பட்டுவிடும்"

## 3. oDYVvqe1gTI — "Badhaka factors" (பாதகாதிபதி காரணிகள்)

Gist: each lagna has a badhaka house and lord; harm comes through that house's and lord's karakatvams; reduce those behaviours to reduce harm. Mostly remedial-behavioural, but gives a complete badhaka table.

Badhaka house used (matches classical: movable → 11th, fixed → 9th, dual → 7th; he names it per lagna, never the rule itself). Note he says for Mithuna "7th is badhaka, not the 10th, Jupiter just also owns 10th".

| Mesha | 11 → Saturn | profit-motive, underpaying/harming workers/servants; paternal uncles (Saturn = step-father karaka), cousin feuds/lawsuits (example: 15-yr case vs uncle) |

| Mithuna | 7 → Jupiter | spouse expectations, wrong money decisions, luxury spending on children, partners, society; **pawned gold never redeemed** |
| Kataka | 11 → Venus | women: elder sister, mother-in-law, older women, **young women** (infatuation ruin), luxury/ostentation |

Derived rules:
- A3.1 Fixed lagnas: "friend turns enemy" theme (9th-lord badhaka).
- A3.2 Badhaka lord's **placement house** adds a channel (Kumbha: Venus in 2nd → harm via speech). Generalisable: badhaka harm flows via badhaka lord's karakas + the house it occupies.
- A3.3 Remedy = avoid the badhaka lord's karakatvams. No dasa-timing rule given.

Compute: `badhaka_house = 11 if lagna_sign in movable (0,3,6,9) else 9 if fixed (1,4,7,10) else 7`; lord via sign rulership; channel = house occupied by that lord. NEW vs engine (engine does no badhaka). Speaker gives no strength/timing weight; use as topic flavour in that lord's dasa/bhukti (my inference, not stated).
