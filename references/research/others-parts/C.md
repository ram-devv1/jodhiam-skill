# Part C — GURUJI TV (Aditya Guruji): Rahu degrees + Jupiter / sama-saptama aspect

Both videos teach Guruji's **subhatva / papatva** (சுபத்துவம் / பாபத்துவம்) and **sukshuma valu** framework. Core
idea: a planet's (or house's) result depends on how much "light" (benefic contact: Jupiter, Venus, waxing/full Moon)
vs "darkness" (Saturn, Mars, Rahu, Sun-combustion) touches it, and **degree closeness decides whether contact counts**.
Raw ASR; numbers spoken in Tamil script sometimes garbled ("6ர டிகிரி" = 6⅔°). Unclear spots are marked.

Direction convention used below (needed for the "approaching vs passed" rule): Rahu moves backward (longitude
decreasing), other planets forward. In the speaker's example Rahu 18°, Mercury 15° = "approaching, not yet crossed",
Venus 24° = "crossed Rahu". So:
- `d = norm180(planet_lon − rahu_lon)`
- `d < 0` (planet behind Rahu in zodiac order) → **approaching** (they are closing on each other)
- `d > 0` (planet ahead of Rahu) → **passed / separating**

(Speaker only gives same-sign examples; the cross-sign handling is inferred from "moves to the next sign → Rahu's
contact fully leaves".)

### Classical texts quoted (video 1)
- **"ஆமேடம் எருது சுறா நண்டு கன்னி ஐந்திடம்"** (verse; Rahu favourable in Aries, Taurus, Capricorn ("சுறா"), Cancer, Virgo) — rejected as unexplained.
- **Uttara Kalamritam (Kalidasa)**: Rahu in 3/6/8/12 "kills" a man; most moola noolgal say Rahu in 3/6/8/12 harmful; "Ketu strongest" — speaker disputes Ketu, says interpolations exist.
- **Uttara Kalamritam** (and "someone before Kalidasa — Brihat Jataka or Parasara Hora"): Rahu in kendra with kona lord / kona with kendra lord → good. Endorsed.
- Unnamed "moolavar": Rahu like Saturn, Ketu like Mars. Endorsed.

### Order of judgement (video 1)
1. Lagna & lagna lord subhatva/papatva first (lagna lord in 8 with Saturn = no effort).
2. Then dasa lords' subhatva.
3. Rahu: who contacts him (Jupiter/Venus vs Saturn/Mars), within degree orbs; sign rules and 3/6/11 don't decide.
4. For planets in Rahu's sign: degree distance + approaching/passed; then other benefic/malefic proximity (Venus/Saturn within 7°), adhi yoga, Jupiter aspect with orb.

### Order of judgement (video 2)
1. "First compute whether this native will have a career at all" → **lagna lord's condition** (G6).
2. Then the 10th house & 10th lord (Saturn in 10 + Jupiter/Venus aspects) (G5).
3. Then dasa-bhukti timing (which dasas arrive when; Chitra-born example: Jupiter dasa ~25–41, Saturn dasa ~41–60 → both "explosive").
4. Karakatva = lifelong character; adhipatya = per dasa (G9). End with lagna lord (G20).
