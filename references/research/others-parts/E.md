### Order of judgement (explicit)
1. Lordship from lagnam (1.1).
2. Dasa lord from lagnam and bhukti lord from lagnam: are they in 6/8/12?
3. Bhukti lord counted from the dasa lord, both ways: 6/8/12? (own-sign exception)
4. Is the bhukti lord retrograde or otherwise afflicted, and is its star lord hidden?
5. Optional: badhaka lord of the dasa lord's sign joining the bhukti lord.
6. Gochara last, as support only.

### Classical verses quoted (source not named; sounds like Jathaga Alangaram style)
- "பங்கமில் ஆட்சி உச்சம் ..." means own/exalted Sun without blemish gives yoga.
- "புந்திதான் உச்ச ராசி தன்னிலே பொருந்த ... சொந்த கேந்திரத்தில் ஆதல் சுகத்தில் கோணத்தாதல் வந்திட வித்தையாகும் வாகனம் ... பொன்னும் திரவியத்தோடும் வாழ்வான்" means Mercury exalted, or in its own sign in a kendra, the 4th or a trine, gives learning, vehicles, gold and wealth.

### How to compute
- `rel(a,b) = ((sign(b) - sign(a)) mod 12) + 1`. Bhukti is bad if `rel(dasa, bhukti) in {6,8,12}` or `rel(bhukti, dasa) in {6,8,12}`. Exception: the hidden planet is in its own sign.
- Shashtashtaka: `rel(dasa, bhukti) in {6,8}` (it is mutual). Weight it more than plain 12.
- The same test fits antaram vs bhukti (the speaker does not say so; this is an extension).
- Badhaka lord of the dasa lord's sign: movable sign uses the 11th lord, fixed the 9th, dual the 7th. Penalty if it is conjunct the bhukti lord.
- Retrograde bhukti lord (`speed < 0`) counts as afflicted.

## 2. 7vpSOvYp4Ds (Astro Whiz / Trichy Arun Subramanian): KP, what a sub lord is

**Gist:** KP splits each nakshatra into 9 unequal subs, in proportion to Vimshottari years, the same way bhuktis split a dasa. This video covers the construction only. It gives **no judgement rules** (span formula and order "next video").

### Rules / construction
| # | Rule | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| 2.1 | Traditional astrology splits a star into 4 equal padas of 3°20'. KP splits it into 9 subs, one per planet. | "ஒவ்வொரு நட்சத்திரத்தையும் ஒன்பது பிரிவுகளா பிரிச்சு" (early) |
| 2.2 | Nakshatra is treated like a dasa, so the subs are cut like bhuktis. | "நட்சத்திரம் தசைக்கு நிகரானது" (early) |
| 2.3 | Bhukti length = dasa years × bhukti-planet years / 120. Worked example for Sun dasa (2160 days at 30-day months): Sun 108 days = 3m18d, Venus 1y, Moon 6m, Mars and Ketu 4m6d each. | "2160/20 ... 108 நாட்கள் ... மூணு மாசம் 18 நாட்கள்" (mid) |
| 2.4 | Sub span = 13°20' × planet years / 120. Sun's sub is the smallest and Venus's (1/6 of the star) the largest. Mars and Ketu subs are equal. The sizes are the same in every star; only the **order changes**. It starts from the star's own lord (example: Krittika, a Sun star, starts with Sun). | "சுக்கிரனோட உபநட்சத்திர அளவு ... ஆறுல ஒரு பங்கு ... இந்த வரிசை ... நட்சத்திரத்துக்கு நட்சத்திரம் மாறும்" (late) |

### How to compute (standard KP; consistent with the video)
```
years = {Ke:7, Ve:20, Su:6, Mo:10, Ma:7, Ra:18, Ju:16, Sa:19, Me:17}; order = Ke Ve Su Mo Ma Ra Ju Sa Me
nak = floor(lon / 13.3333); star_lord = order[nak % 9]
pos = lon - nak*13.3333; walk the order starting at star_lord, span_i = 13.3333*years_i/120 until pos falls inside -> sub lord
```

### How to compute ashtakavarga (needed by sections 3 and 4; standard Parashari BAV tables, not given in either video)
