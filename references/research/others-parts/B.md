# Part B: GURUJI TV (Aditya Guruji), 3 videos

All three videos use the speaker's **subhathuvam / papathuvam** (benefic vs malefic influence) system. Its core idea: a planet's result depends on the benefic or malefic "light" (aspect, conjunction, Moon's phase) reaching it. Its sign or star matters much less. ASR is noisy. Places I could not resolve are marked *(unclear)*.

### Order of judgement (this video)
1. Compute subhathuvam/papathuvam layers per planet: who aspects or conjoins it, Moon's phase, closest degree.
2. Then lordship from lagna, plus "acts like" for Rahu/Ketu (sign lord / conjunct planet).
3. Then dasa → bhukti, using the bhukti lord's kendra/kona/12th position from the dasa lord.

5. Ignore stars entirely, except Moon's star for dasa computation.

### Order of judgement
Saturn: check dignity first (exalted, own sign or debilitated?). Then hidden in 6/8/12, or in kendra/kona? Then retrograde? Then benefic aspect (Guru/Venus/lone Mercury), then benefic conjunction. Then read lordship by lagna and karakas by subhathuvam.

### Classical texts
Only "விளக்க நூல்கள்" (commentary texts) are cited for the vakra-equals-opposite-dignity rule. The speaker cites his own book series "ஜோதிடம் எனும் தேவரகசியம்".

## 4. NEW vs engine: ranked, with compute recipes

Inputs: sidereal longitude L[p], speed S[p], lagna. Sign = floor(L/30). Aspects (whole-sign, needed by most rules): all planets aspect the 7th sign; Mars also aspects the 4th and 8th, Guru the 5th and 9th, Saturn the 3rd and 10th. The speaker also treats Rahu/Ketu as aspecting (implied, not stated).

1. **Combustion with a 1° aspect cut-off + Sun inherits the light** (P1, P2, P3, P7).
   - `combust(p) = |L[p]−L[Sun]| < classical orb` (the speaker gives no wide orb).
   - Guru's aspect is reduced only when `|ΔL| ≤ 1°`.
   - When Guru is combust: mark the Sun as subha in its dasa, even as an 8th lord.
   - Cancellation: Moon within the 6–8 sign range from the planet AND Moon−Sun elongation ≈ 180° (full Moon), OR Venus within 10–15° of the planet and Venus not itself combust.
2. **Moon phase (paksha strength) overrides the nakshatra** (B3, B4, B7, B17).
   - `elong = (L[Moon]−L[Sun]) mod 360`.
   - Full (pournami) ≈ 168–192° (tithi 14–16; the speaker says pournami without a number).
   - Amavasai ≈ <12° or >348°. Ashtami/navami ≈ 84–108° or 252–276°.
   - A full-Moon aspect or conjunction flips a malefic to strongly good (e.g. Mars giving Mangala yoga); for Mars in the 10th, read his lordships through the 10th.
3. **Subhathuvam/papathuvam scoring by aspect and conjunction** (B9, B10, B11, X10).
   - Benefic sources: Guru, Venus, full Moon, lone Mercury. Malefic sources: Saturn, Mars, Rahu, new Moon.
   - Weight an aspect from a benefic above a conjunction (for malefic targets).
   - If both malefic and benefic touch a planet: its dasa is bad first, and good in the last ~3 years.
   - When several malefics aspect, the one with the smallest degree difference dominates.
4. **Conjunction orb = within one nakshatra (13°20'), "8–11°"** (B8). Same sign AND `|ΔL| ≤ ~11–13.3°`; otherwise it is a weak conjunction.
5. **Rahu in the 6th: alone = protective; with Saturn in the 6th AND a Mars aspect = severe 6th-house trouble** (B10). This modifies the engine's "Rahu good in 3/6/11" by adding the triple-malefic exception.
6. **Retrograde only with dignity** (X1, X2, X4, X9). `retro = S[p] < 0`.
   - Exalted + retro → treat as near-debilitated, i.e. a weakened malefic. For Saturn that is GOOD (karaka harm removed, lordship delivered), especially in kendra/kona.
   - Debilitated + retro → near-exalted. For Saturn that is BAD (karakas amplified).
   - Retrograde in other signs: ignore.
7. **Malefic strong = bad for Saturn: hide or soften** (X3, X4, X13). Exalted, own-sign or moolatrikona Saturn is good only if:
   - in the 6/8/12 from lagna (sookshma valu), OR
   - with Ketu (full sookshma valu), OR with Mars (partial), OR
   - retrograde and aspected by Guru/Venus/Mercury.
8. **Saturn level-4: conjunct Guru, with Guru in Dhanus/Meena best** → spirituality flag plus Saturn trades (X11).
9. **Damage to the occupied house:** Rahu 100%, Saturn 60–70% unless Guru aspects Saturn (then Saturn grows the house). Saturn's aspect = lordship good + karaka bad (B14, B15, B16).
10. **Bhukti judged from the dasa lord:** the bhukti lord in kendra/kona from the dasa lord carries the dasa's theme. A bhukti lord in the 12th from the dasa lord, if strong and well placed from lagna, relieves it. Marriage comes in bhuktis of planets in kendra/kona from the dasa lord (B13, B18). Compute `(sign[bhukti] − sign[dasa]) mod 12 + 1`.
11. **Stability check:** lagna lord in own or exalted sign, and no planets in the 6th/8th from the lagna lord's sign → "no big falls" (B19).
12. **8th and 12th both subha → lives far from home** (B20, P4). Lagna lord placed in the 8th while also the 10th lord → career abroad (P6).
13. **Ketu "acts like" his sign lord AND receives that lord's aspect;** a good Ketu gives ~80% of 12th results plus 7th results (B17). This extends the engine's Rahu/Ketu sign-lord rule.

**Conflict with engine:** the engine uses star-lord signification for house topics. This speaker rejects star-lord results entirely, even for Rahu/Ketu, and keeps Moon's star only for dasa. Consider gating star-lord logic behind a "school" flag.
