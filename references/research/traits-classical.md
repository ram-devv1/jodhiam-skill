# Character, behaviour and lifestyle from the chart: classical sources

Research notes for the "Commonly observed behaviours & lifestyle" section of a one-person reading.
Pure traditional astrology. Every row cites a text and chapter. Rewordings are modern and plain; harsh
or archaic wording ("thief", "enamoured of others' wives") is softened to its behavioural core but
kept as a negative. Uncertain items are marked **[?]**.

Researched 2026-11-23. No agentyc used. Source texts were downloaded and read directly (list at the end).

### 1.2 Recommended weighting (synthesis; where it comes from)

Use this order. Higher items set the theme; lower items add colour or exceptions.

1. **Lagnam sign** (M1, M17): body, outward manner, how the person acts. Fixed base.
2. **Lagna lord: dignity + house** (M6, M16, M20): turns the lagnam's traits up or down. Strong lagna lord
   (own/exalted/friendly, kendra/trikona) = the positive version of the lagnam list. Weak or in 6/8/12 = the negative version.
3. **Planets in the lagnam, then planets aspecting it** (M5, §2.4): override the sign where they conflict (Mars in lagnam
   makes even a soft sign quick-tempered). Benefic aspect softens a malefic's edge (ESH rule 15: "கனிந்த நெருப்பு").
4. **Moon sign (rasi)** (M2, M3, M12, M15): inner mind, moods, food and comfort habits, how they react. Same
   sign list as lagnam (M3), so use §2.3 for both. If a planet joins or aspects the Moon, that planet's nature dominates (M15).
5. **Janma nakshatram** (M13, M14, M18): specific habits and quirks. Weight by Moon strength.
6. **Pada → navamsa sign → pada lord** (M19, HS 30): fine-tunes the star. Tamil practice; classical support is indirect
   (SAR 9.45 & 51.1 use the Moon's navamsa lord for appearance/disposition).
7. **Strongest planet** (M8): gives the dominant "flavour" and look. Use when one planet is clearly strongest (exalted in a kendra, etc.).
8. **4th lord** (mind, home, peace) and **5th lord** (intelligence, judgement): use only for the "home life" and
   "how they think/study" lines, from `notes-lords-1-6.md`.
9. **Sun sign**: minor in Tamil practice. Use only for pride/authority/father tone. (SAR ch.22, BJ ch.18 lists are
   mostly about status and fortune, not temperament.) **[?] low weight is our convention, not a text rule.**
10. **Atmakaraka** (Jaimini): **not used** by the jothidar method in this skill and not mentioned in any source above.
    Skip for the one-person reading; if added, keep to one line ("soul's lesson"), marked as a Jaimini add-on.

### 1.3 Where texts and teachers disagree

- **Lagnam vs Moon: which is "outer" and which "inner"?** Classical texts do **not** make this split. They treat
  the two lists as the same (PD 9.13, BPHS 12.11) and say use the **stronger** (HS 31). The "lagnam = outer behaviour,
  Moon = inner mind" split is a modern convention (common on astrology websites, e.g. AstroVed blog
  "ascendant-vs-moon-sign"). It is a useful writing device that doesn't contradict the texts, as long as a
  much stronger Moon is allowed to take the lead.
- **Body/appearance:** BPHS uses the lagnam sign; Saravali prefers the navamsa lagna or the strongest planet or the
  Moon's navamsa lord (SAR 9.45, 51.1). Practical: describe build from lagnam + planets in it; describe complexion
  and "look" only loosely.
- **Nakshatra lists disagree with each other.** Varahamihira (BJ16/BS101) and Prithuyasas (HS28) often differ:
  Bharani (BJ: truthful, healthy, happy; HS: unsteady thinking, chases romance), Pushya (BJ: calm, self-controlled;
  HS: very angry, bold), Hasta (BJ: shameless, drinker, thief; HS: clever, good speaker, lives abroad), Chitra (BJ:
  only looks and dress; HS: argumentative, likes bad company). The tables below give both and a merged plain line;
  where they clash, the merged line keeps what both share and drops the extreme.
- **Translation quirk:** Neely's English for BJ 16.6 labels the second star "Uttara Phalguni"; by order (and in Iyer's
  BS101) it is **Purva Phalguni (Pooram)**. Used as Pooram below.
- **Pada traits:** modern websites give per-pada traits that disagree a lot with each other (e.g. Rohini pada 1:
  "calm and gentle" vs "highly ambitious"; astrosage vs rahasyavedicastrology). Don't use them. Use the pada-lord method (§2.2b).
- **Moon strength rule:** BS101 (Moon powerful), HS28 (benefic aspect on the star), HS29 (no planet joining/aspecting)
  and ESH (no Saturn/Rahu, waxing) are four versions of the same idea. Use ESH's concrete test; it is a subset of all four.

#### 2.2b Pada notes (pada → navamsa sign → pada lord)

Navamsa sign of a pada: star number n (0 = Aswini), pada p (0–3) → sign index (4n + p) mod 12 from Mesham.
Shortcut: stars 1, 4, 7, … (Aswini, Rohini, Punarpoosam, Magam, Hastham, Visakam, Moolam, Thiruvonam,
Poorattathi) start at **Mesham**; stars 2, 5, 8, … (Bharani, Mirugasirisham, Poosam, Pooram, Chithirai, Anusham,
Pooradam, Avittam, Uthirattathi) start at **Simmam**; stars 3, 6, 9, … (Karthigai, Thiruvathirai, Ayilyam, Uthiram,
Swathi, Kettai, Uthiradam, Sathayam, Revathi) start at **Dhanusu**. Pada 1 → that sign, pada 2 → next sign, etc.
(ESH Part D §1.1–1.2; matches standard navamsa.)

## 3. How to combine into one short section (no contradictions)

### 3.1 Steps

1. **Pick the lead.** Compare lagna lord strength vs Moon strength (own/exalted/friendly sign, kendra/trikona, waxing
   Moon, benefic aspect). Normally the lagnam leads (M1, M17). If the Moon is clearly stronger and the lagna lord weak,
   let the Moon sign lead (M4, HS 31).
2. **Outer vs inner (writing device).** Write lagnam traits as *how they come across / act* and Moon sign + star as
   *how they feel / react / what they need*. This matches the modern convention and doesn't break the texts (§1.3).
3. **Set intensity from dignity.** Strong lord → write the positive version of its list; weak, in 6/8/12 or with
   malefics → write the negative version (BPHS 12.1–3, 24; BJ 17.13). Dignity changes **how much**, not **which** traits.
4. **Let a joining planet override.** Planet in lagnam, or planet joined with/aspecting the Moon, replaces the sign's
   tone where they clash (BPHS 12.4; HS 29). Saturn on the Moon = serious, worried; Jupiter = wise, calm; Mars = quick temper.
5. **Add star and pada as specifics.** Take 1–2 habits from the nakshatra and one tone from the pada lord. Apply the
   ESH fit test: Moon free of Saturn/Rahu and waxing → state plainly; otherwise hedge ("partly").
6. **Resolve conflicts by agreement, not averaging.** Keep traits that appear in **two or more layers** (e.g. Mesham
   lagnam + Bharani + Simmam pada all say "quick, proud"). Drop single-layer extremes. When two layers truly clash
   (calm lagnam, angry star), write it as a pattern: "calm on the surface, quick to flare when pushed".
7. **Strongest planet** gives the closing "dominant flavour" line (SAR 9.45).

### 3.2 Lifestyle lines: where each comes from

| Area | Read from (first → last) |
|---|---|
| Looks / build | Lagnam sign, planets in lagnam, strongest planet (BPHS 11.2, 12.4; SAR 9.45) |
| Temperament | Lagnam + lagna lord, then Moon sign and aspects on the Moon |
| Food | 2nd house + Moon sign/star cues (eats little: Mesham Moon, Aswini; big appetite: Mithunam Moon, Karthigai; rich food/meat: Simmam; fluids/water: Punarpoosam, Swathi, Meenam lagnam) |
| Sleep / rest | 12th house and its lord; Moon condition (afflicted Moon = poor sleep) |
| Money habits | 2nd and 11th lords; Moon/star cues (saver: Kanni, Uthirattathi; spender: Rishabam lagnam, Pooram, Thiruvonam; tight: Visakam, Poorattathi, Dhanusu Moon) |
| Social style | Lagnam + 11th; star cues (few friends: Aswini, Kettai; many friends: Uthiradam, Pooradam loyal) |
| Relationships | 7th house and lord, Venus; star cues (spouse leads: Poorattathi, Meenam Moon) |
| Work style | 10th lord, Saturn (discipline), Mars (drive), Sun (authority), Mercury (skill); star cues (trader: Swathi, Thulam; leader: Magam, Uthiram) |

### 3.3 Writing rules

- 5–8 bullets max; each bullet is one behaviour, with one strength and (where true) one caution.
- Every negative gets a softer modern word ("blunt", "careful with money", "keeps a private side") and, if useful, a
  "do" line, matching `method.md` ("Keep this short and balanced: one strength and one weakness").
- Never print the harsh classical words ("thief", "cruel", "shameless"). They become "bends rules under pressure", "can be cutting", "unfiltered".
- Don't stack the same trait three times from three layers; say it once, stronger.
- Twins check: if the chart-twins study (SKILL.md §5) disagrees with a trait, keep the chart trait but soften it.

### 3.4 Worked micro-example (method only, invented chart)

Lagnam Simmam, Sun (lagna lord) in the 10th in Mesham (exalted); Moon in Mesham, Bharani pada 1 (Simmam navamsa),
waxing, no Saturn/Rahu contact; Mars aspects the Moon.
- Lead = lagnam (lord exalted in a kendra). Outer: proud, commanding, speaks little but firmly (Simmam, PD9/SAR48), positive version.
- Inner: quick, brave, restless (Mesham Moon, BJ17); Mars aspect → bold, quick temper (SAR 23).
- Star: determined, honest, persistent (Bharani, BJ16; ESH). Pada Simmam (Sun) → pride, wants first place.
- Agreement across 4 layers: **pride + quick temper + leadership**. Write it once: "Natural leader who expects to be
  in charge; temper flares fast but passes. Do: count to five before replying."
