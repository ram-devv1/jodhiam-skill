## 3. Order of judgement (as close as the speaker gets)

None of the six videos gives a formal numbered procedure. The sequence that recurs:
1. Longevity first, then yogas (8L video, explicit).
2. Base result = lord of X in house Y (or any X–Y "connection").
3. The lord's dignity in Y (own/exalted/debilitated/friend/enemy), then whether it is a natural benefic or malefic.
4. Aspects and conjunctions on the lord and on house Y (benefic vs malefic, and the aspecting planet's strength).
5. Lord of house Y: hidden in 6/8/12? in a kendra? debilitated?
6. Karaka of the matter, plus the lagna/lagna lord and Jupiter as general buffers.
7. Timing in the lord's dasa/bhukti; the relocation remedy.
8. For character traits, combine with lagna and the 4th before predicting (9L in 12).

## 4. What's NEW vs the engine (method.md / predict.py), ranked

planet-in-house text, house signification via own/occupy/star lord.

   keyed `[lordOf][placedIn]` -> {verdict, lines}. Compute: lordOf = house whose sign the planet rules (two per planet),
   placedIn = (planetSign − lagnaSign) mod 12 + 1. This is the biggest gap. The engine has planet-in-house only.
2. **X–Y "connection" link (P1).** Treat as linked if the X-lord is in Y, the Y-lord is in X, they exchange signs,

3. **Medical-career signature (P10).** If an 8–10 or 2–8 link exists and lagna lord, Sun and Mars are all
   strong (own/exalted/friendly sign, not in 6/8/12) -> "medicine/surgery". If they are weak -> "unconventional/hidden trade".
4. **Kendra/kona/upachaya lord-placement scoring (P3).** Score by (class of house owned) × (class of house occupied):
   trikona lord in a kendra or trikona ++, kendra lord in a trikona ++, upachaya lord in a trikona +,
   lords of 9/10/11 in 6/8/12 −−. This adds to the current functional-benefic score.
5. **Buffers (P7, P8, P9).** Soften a bad lord placement when (a) the lagna lord is strong, (b) the lord of the

6. **Lord's own 7th aspect (P5).** If the lord's 7th aspect lands on its own house, add +. If an 8L/12L aspects

- After lagna, 7th must be strongest house ("லக்னத்திற்கு அடுத்தபடியாக ஏழாம் பாவகம் நல்லா வலிமையா இருக்கணும்").
- Business partners; strong 7th for partnership business ("தொழில் கூட்டாளிகளை").
- Opposite sex; competitors/rivals ("போட்டியாளர்களை").
- Marriage: when, whether, happiness, problems.
- Comforts/luxury, adornment, neighbours, relatives visiting, trade, travel/pilgrimage, going abroad/other state.
- Marital/sexual pleasure and disturbance ("தாம்பத்திய சுகம், தாம்பத்திய கலக்கம்").
- Body: kidneys ("சிறுநீரகத்தை குறிக்கக்கூடிய இடம்"); spinal cord, back/hip pain when 7th afflicted ("ஸ்பைனல் கார்டு ... இடுப்பு வலி ... முதுகு வலி").
- Venereal disease, sensual pleasure, lust, good deeds ("பால்வினை நோய்களை", "நற்காரியங்களை").
- Maraka sthanam (see B).
- Love from spouse, spouse's morals, gifts / sudden luck / unexpected gift ("திடீர் அதிஷ்டம்").
- Adopted son ("வளர்ப்பு மகனை குறிக்கக்கூடிய ஸ்தானம்") — for those without children who adopt.
- Romantic love feelings ("காதல் உணர்வு").
- Strong, subha 7th => chart definitely rises ("சுபத்துவமா ... உயர்வையும் ஒரு மாற்றத்தையும் நோக்கி டெபனட்டா பயணிக்கும்").

## 0. Intro: significations of 8th house (early, c0)
- Longevity (ஆயுள் பலம்) is primary: "எட்டாம் பாவகம் இல்லைன்னா ஜாதகத்தில எத்தனை யோகம் இருந்தாலும் அது பயனற்றது" — even Pancha Mahapurusha yogas useless without longevity.
- Mangalya balam (esp. women's chart): "தீர்க்க சுமங்கல்யா மாங்கல்ய பலத்தோட இருப்பாங்களா".
- Nature of death: untimely vs natural ("அகால ... அல்லது இயற்கை மரணமா").
- 6th vs 8th contrast: 6 = defeat / curable disease / debt; 8 = irrecoverable defeat ("மீள முடியாத தோல்வி"), incurable disease ("தீராத நோய்", long-term tablets), huge unrepayable debt ("கோடி கோடியா கடன்").

- Names: ஜீவன ஸ்தானம், கர்ம ஸ்தானம், உத்தியோக ஸ்தானம், தொழில் ஸ்தானம்.

- 10th must be at least moderately strong for good livelihood and social respect.
