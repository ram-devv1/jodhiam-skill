## 4. Order of judgement (as stated; nothing more formal exists in these videos)

1. Find the lagna → count to the house → its lord → which house from lagna the lord occupies (L2 explicit).
2. Read the general placement result.
3. Adjust: lagna-wise benefic/malefic status of the lord → dignity in that sign (own/exalt/debil/friend/enemy/neutral/moolatrikona) → retrograde / combust → conjunctions and aspects (malefic cuts, benefic lifts) → Rahu–Ketu grip (L1 mid, L2 end, L6 end).
4. Cross-check with related lords and karakas (lagna lord and 2L for the 6L-in-1 case; 7L/11th/Venus/Mars for marriage; Mercury for education).
5. Timing: results come in the lord's dasa-bhukti; speculation only in a clean yoga dasa.
6. Calibrate: lord alone ≈ 50% fit; malefic influence −30%; benefic lord fuller.

## Intro (what lagna shows), early
- Lagna = head (சிரசு) of the chart, the starting point; most important. "லக்னம் லக்னாதிபதி வலுவா இருந்தாதான் அந்த ஜாதகத்துல இருக்கக்கூடிய யோகங்களை ... அனுபவிக்க முடியும்" — if lagna/lord weak, all other yogas are "useless"; if strong, native fights through and wins even without yogas. Chart's "mark/score" decided by lagna + lord strength.
- Lagna signifies: character, complexion, appearance, body build, life capacity/activity, name/fame, longevity (தீர்க்க vs மத்திம ஆயுள்), disease-free life, head/hair/face, thinking/intelligence, mindset, type of money loss (பணவிரயம்), honour, morality (weak lagna/lord → ஒழுக்க கேடு), devotion (also check 5th & 9th).
- Lords listed: Mars (Mesha/Vrischika), Venus (Rishaba/Thula), Mercury (Mithuna/Kanni), Moon (Kataka), Sun (Simha), Jupiter (Dhanus/Meena), Saturn (Makara/Kumbha).

## Context: what the 5th house signifies (early, ~5–22%)
- பூர்வ புண்ணிய / புத்திர / புத்தி ஸ்தானம். It is the "small trikona" (திரிகோண ஸ்தானத்தில ஒரு சிறிய கோணம்). 1, 5 and 9 create the following generations (வம்ச விருத்தி).
- Children (birth, their condition), past-life merit and sin, intuition and soul strength, the deep mind (good or bad thoughts), happiness, music, literature and art, planning mindset, love affairs.
- Intelligence: if the 5th is strong, the person has experiential wisdom even without schooling, learns easily and has a research mindset.
- Luck: lottery, race, shares, income without labour. It is 2nd from the 4th, so rental income from vehicles and houses.
- Ancestral property, ancestral trade, political influence, administrative skill, name and fame, enjoyment (உல்லாசம்), staying in or leaving the native place, whether efforts succeed.
- Kuladeivam (family deity) grace. The 5th house AND Saturn must both be strong, because Saturn is the karaka for the kuladeivam ("குலதெய்வத்துக்கு காரகரா வரக்கூடியவர் சனி").
- If the 5th is spoiled, childbirth is delayed or obstructed, or the children bring mental strain, sorrow and expense. The 5th "must be strong, at least minimally strong".
