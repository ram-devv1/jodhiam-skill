## 3. Order of judgement (as stated)

There is no single explicit numbered procedure. The implied steps, repeated across videos:
1. General result of planet X in house N (the "pothu palan").
2. Planet's state: dignity, dig bala/nishbala/sthana bala, retrograde, combust, waxing/waning (Moon).
3. Contacts: malefic vs benefic conjunction/aspect, with **Guru's aspect as the override**. Check whether the planet is alone.
4. The house lord and dispositor: their placement (in 6/8/12 or not; own/exalted), especially for 4th (mother), 5th (children) and 9th (father).
5. Lagna and lagna lord strength (and 1/5/9 lords) scale the final intensity.
6. Lagna-specific lordship (functional benefic/malefic, VRY, yoga karaka): "each lagna reacts differently".
7. Timing: the effect fires in the planet's own dasa, bhukti or antaram.

Explicit sub-procedure for putra dosham (Jupiter in 5, 7_94 mid): "ஐந்தாம் அதிபதியின் நிலை என்ன? ஐந்தாம் அதிபதி எந்த இடத்தில அமர்ந்திருக்காரு அவருக்கு வீடு கொடுத்தவர் எந்த இடத்தில அமர்ந்திருக்காரு. ஐந்தாம் இடத்தில பாவகிரங்குடைய சேர்க்கையோ பார்வையோ இருக்கா?" That is: 5th lord's state, then 5th lord's house, then its dispositor's house, then malefic contact on the 5th.
For education (Ketu in 1): check the Sun–Mercury conjunction plus the strength of the 4th and 9th houses.

## 4. New vs engine: ranked rules to encode

Inputs: sidereal longitudes L[p], speeds, lagna degree. sign(p)=floor(L/30), house(p)=(sign(p)-sign(lagna))%12+1.

1. **Guru-aspect cancellation.** If Jupiter aspects (house 5, 7 or 9 from Jupiter, whole-sign) the house of a malefic or dosham placement (Rahu/Ketu axis, Mars dosham, Maandhi, Saturn on the Moon), drop or halve its negative score. `aspects(Ju, h) = ((h - house(Ju)) % 12 + 1) in {5,7,9}`; conjunction counts too.
2. **Rahu/Ketu = sign lord + star lord.** star = floor(L*27/360); star lord from the Vimshottari order. Blend the natural/functional nature of the dispositor and the star lord, then add conjunctions. Add **10 (and 9 for Rahu)** to the good houses; boost when the dispositor is strong (own/exalt/kendra-trikona).
3. **Sun–Rahu orb.** If Sun and Rahu are in the same sign: d = |L_Sun - L_Rahu| (wrap to 180). If d is small, apply the grahana dosham (father/health/govt; severe if the 9th lord is in 6/8/12). If d ≥ ~20°, apply the yoga version. The "close" cutoff is unspecified, so suggest < 10° as a knob. Trigger only in Sun or Rahu dasa/bhukti.
